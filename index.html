Chapter 05 · A letter for you

Dear Bittu ❤️

Bichhadte hain... tootte hain... gussa hote hain... kabhi-kabhi ek dusre se door bhi chale jaate hain. Lekin pata nahi hum dono mein aisa kya hai, ki kitni bhi baar sab kuch ulajh jaaye, aakhir mein hum phir ek dusre ke paas aa hi jaate hain. Shayad isi ko apna hona kehte hain. Tumse baat karke din achha ho jaata hai, aur tumse baat kiye bina din thoda adhura sa lagta hai. Aur neend... Neend sabse achhi tumhare saath hi aati hai. Chahe tum mere paas ho ya mujhse bahut door... Agar tum door hoti ho, toh bhi call par tumhe saath lekar so jaata hoon. Phone ke doosri taraf tum hoti ho, aur is taraf mujhe lagta hai jaise tum paas hi ho. Mujhe nahi pata future humare liye kya likh kar rakha hai. Bas itna pata hai... Har baar jab hum toot-te hain, mera dil tumhe hi dhoondhta hai. Har baar jab hum ladte hain, andar se mann karta hai ki bas tum maan jao. Aur har baar jab tum wapas aati ho, lagta hai... haan, yahi toh meri jagah hai. Tum perfect nahi ho. Main bhi nahi hoon. Hum dono kabhi-kabhi pagal hain, kabhi stubborn hain, kabhi unnecessarily ladte hain... Lekin shayad isi imperfect story mein hum dono ka sabse beautiful part chhupa hai. Bas ek baat yaad rakhna... Doori chahe jitni ho, gussa chahe jitna ho, life chahe jitni complicated ho... Tum mere liye hamesha "someone special" nahi rahogi. Tum meri favourite person rahogi. ❤️
— Yours, always ❤️
